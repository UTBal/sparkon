FROM SOFA TO SHOWDOWN

One room.
Everyone’s invited.

  1. Make it yours.

    One person creates a room on their phone. Share the room code with your players.

  2. Gather your players.

    Friends join on their phones. The host approves them and pairs up to two TVs, even in different homes.

  3. Let curiosity win.

    Start when you’re ready. Pick the science, lock your answer and see how everyone did.

Accounts and fair play

Guest progress is temporary. Google sign-in is available to save your collection. Screen styles are cosmetic; your cards keep their actual editions and scoring is identical.

Current game scoring runs in the host’s browser. Competitive rewards and card transfers are not available.
