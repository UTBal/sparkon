Biology ◆ Rare: a famous discovery

Photosynthesis

Swedish: Fotosyntes · Discovered: Jan Ingenhousz showed light is needed, 1779

Plants use light to build sugar from carbon dioxide and water, and give off oxygen.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Why it works

A tree does not get heavy by eating soil. The carbon in the wood comes from carbon dioxide in the air; light energy is stored in the sugar.

The oxygen you breathe was released by plants and algae. Burning wood or food runs photosynthesis backwards and gets the energy out again.

What it lets you predict

Most of a tree's mass comes from the air.

How tests ask about it

  • The balanced formula

Try it

A small tree gains 50 kg over ten years, but the soil in its pot is almost unchanged. Where did the mass come from?
