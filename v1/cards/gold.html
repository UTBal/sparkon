Chemistry ★ Legendary: one of the biggest ideas in science

Gold

Swedish: Guld · Discovered: Oldest known gold jewellery: Varna, Bulgaria, about 4500 BC · symbol from Latin aurum

Gold is a noble metal: it hardly reacts, so it never rusts and stays shiny for thousands of years.

Au · atomic number 79 · ρ = 19.3 g/cm³

Why it works

Legendary card because gold is rare: only about 4 grams in every 1,000 tonnes of Earth's crust.

Pure gold is 24 carat. An 18 carat ring is 18/24 = 75 % gold, mixed with other metals to make it harder.

What it lets you predict

Treasure found after 3,000 years still shines.

How tests ask about it

  • Reactivity: why gold does not rust
  • Carats as percent

Try it

An 18 carat ring weighs 10 g. How much of it is gold?

Show the answer

18 / 24 = 0.75, so 0.75 × 10 = 7.5 g of gold.
