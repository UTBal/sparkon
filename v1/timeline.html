When each idea was found

School teaches the ideas in a tidy order. People found them in a messy one: gold was known long before anyone knew what an element was, and a Babylonian scribe used Pythagoras' theorem over a thousand years before Pythagoras.

4500 BC · Chemistry
Gold · Oldest known gold jewellery: Varna, Bulgaria, about 4500 BC · symbol from Latin aurum
1800 BC · Math
Pythagoras' theorem · Babylon, about 1800 BC · proved by Greeks about 500 BC
250 BC · Chemistry
Density · Archimedes, about 250 BC
14 · Math
Percent change · Roman taxes, about AD 14 (per centum = per hundred)
1687 · Physics
Newton's second law · Isaac Newton, 1687
1772 · Chemistry
Oxygen · Carl Wilhelm Scheele (Sweden), about 1772
1779 · Biology
Photosynthesis · Jan Ingenhousz showed light is needed, 1779
1827 · Physics
Ohm's law · Georg Ohm, 1827
1847 · Physics
Energy conservation · Mayer, Joule and Helmholtz, 1840s
1953 · Biology
DNA · Structure: Franklin, Watson, Crick and Wilkins, 1953
