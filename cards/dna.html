Biology ★ Legendary: one of the biggest ideas in science

DNA

Swedish: DNA · Discovered: Structure: Franklin, Watson, Crick and Wilkins, 1953

DNA is a code of four letters on a twisted ladder, and each half tells you the other.

A pairs with T · C pairs with G

Why it works

The ladder's rungs are pairs of bases: A always with T, C always with G. Split the ladder down the middle and each half is a template for building a new partner.

Rosalind Franklin's X-ray photo showed the twisted shape; Watson and Crick used it to build the model.

What it lets you predict

One strand gives you the other strand.

How tests ask about it

  • Write the partner strand
  • How a cell copies DNA
