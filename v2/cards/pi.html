The π Mystery

Swedish: Talet pi · Discovered: Egypt and Babylon, about 1650 BC · pinned down by Archimedes, about 250 BC

Every circle in the universe, from a coin to a planet's orbit, goes round exactly π times its width.

π = C ÷ d = 3.14159…

Why it works

Measure round a plate, then across it. Divide. You get a little over 3. Do it with a bike wheel, a lake, the orbit of the Moon: the same number every time. That number is π.

Its digits never end and never repeat: people have worked out trillions of them and found no pattern. That was proved in 1761. So every circle hides a number nobody will ever write down in full.

The π card is the Eureka mystery card. Only one will ever be made: brushed metal, with π in real gold foil and its digits running round the edge. It is hidden in one random booster pack. Whoever finds it can register it here with its serial number.

Try it

Show the answer
