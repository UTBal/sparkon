Ohm's law

Swedish: Ohms lag · Discovered: Georg Ohm, 1827

The current through a resistor grows in step with the voltage across it.

U = R · I

Why it works

Voltage is the push, current is the flow, resistance is how narrow the pipe is. Double the push and you double the flow.

Resistors in series simply add up: 10 Ω and 20 Ω in a row act like one 30 Ω resistor.

What it lets you predict

The current before you switch on.

How tests ask about it

  • Find current, voltage or resistance
  • Resistors in series

Try it

A 9 V battery is connected to a 30 Ω resistor. What is the current?

Show the answer

I = U / R = 9 / 30 = 0.3 A.
